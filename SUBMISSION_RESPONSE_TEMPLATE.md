# Submission Response Template

Use this when replying to someone who submitted an event for the Art Basil calendar.

---

Hi [Name],

Thanks so much for submitting [Event Title] to the Art Basil SF Bay Area calendar!

I've added it to the site: [brief description of what was added — exhibition dates, venue, etc.]

You can check out the listing here: [Art Basil SF](https://artbasil.info/sf/index.html)

Thanks again for sharing, and congrats on the show!

Warmest regards,
Art Basil

---

Swap `https://artbasil.info/sf/index.html` for the matching region page (`/la/`, `/mtl/`, `/tor/`) when responding to submissions outside the SF Bay Area.
